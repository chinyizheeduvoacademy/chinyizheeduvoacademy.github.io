About The Curator

Chin Yi Zhe

Software Engineering • Offensive Security Researcher

I studied for a Professional Diploma in Software Engineering at Eduvo Academy. The academic promise was familiar: finish the coursework, land a mandatory six-month industry internship, and step straight into a junior engineering role.

That internship never happened. I never landed it. The pipeline collapsed, companies ghosted, and I discovered early that paper certificates without verifiable proof of work have zero currency in this industry.

To be completely transparent: I've been on both sides of the coin. I know how the underground works—the telegram leak groups, the grey-market brokers, and dark-web dumps. And I know how broken conventional computer science degrees are, charging students thousands of dollars for textbook theory while ignoring real adversarial mechanics. In the real world, nobody cares what school stamped your paper. They only care if you can reverse the binary, trace the execution chain, and discover the vulnerability before an adversary weaponizes it.

So I stopped waiting for an academic pipeline to validate me. Working from Kuala Lumpur, I focused on offensive security engineering—reverse engineering ARM surveillance firmware, finding and disclosing CVEs, and writing low-footprint tooling in Go and Python under github.com/chinyizheeduvoacademy.

The most liberating truth I learned was that every single resource that gave me real technical competence was 100% free and official: PortSwigger's Web Security Academy, OverTheWire Bandit, TCM's free TryHackMe rooms, LiveOverflow, and Jeremy's CCNA. The only barrier was knowing where to look and what sequence to follow without drowning in information overload.

LvxPaths is the open roadmap platform I needed when I had zero dollars, no internship, and a mountain of unorganized browser tabs. No paywalls, no mandatory logins, and zero corporate gatekeeping.
